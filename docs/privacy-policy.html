Privacy Policy for Gym Nerd

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Gym Nerd ("we", "us", "our") is a workout tracking app designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is treated.

1. Your workout data stays on your device

All data you enter in Gym Nerd — workouts, sets, reps, weights, custom metrics, RPE, discomfort notes, execution notes, and settings — is stored only in a local database on your own device.

  • We operate no servers, no accounts, and no cloud sync.
  • Your workout data is never transmitted to us or to any third party by the app.
  • Your data is deleted from the device if you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

2. Crash reports

To fix bugs, the app may send anonymous crash reports via Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) when the app crashes or encounters an error. These reports contain technical diagnostics only, such as:

  • Device model, operating system version, and app version;
  • The error message and stack trace.

Crash reports do not contain your workouts, notes, or any other content you entered. Sentry may process this technical data on servers outside your country (including the United States) and retains it according to its own retention schedule (currently around 90 days). See sentry.io/privacy for details.

3. Sharing you initiate

The app lets you export or share files (for example, sending your history to a trainer). Content leaves your device only when you explicitly choose to share it, using the standard Android share sheet, and only to the recipient you select.

4. What we do NOT do

  • No analytics, advertising SDKs, or cross-app trackers.
  • No sale, rental, or sharing of personal data with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

5. Permissions

The app requests only the permissions needed to run and to save or share files you explicitly export. No permission is used to collect data about you.

6. Children

Gym Nerd is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Since all data stays on-device, there is nothing for a parent to request deletion of from us; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

7. Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, OS updates). We encourage keeping your device secured.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL and the "Last updated" date revised. Material changes will also be noted in the app's store listing where feasible.
